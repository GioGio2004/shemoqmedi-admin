Welcome to the team. Shemoqmedi is small, so everything you touch is close to production. This page gives you a reading order for your first week and the handful of rules that keep the live site, the money and people's trust safe.

## The rules

These matter more than anything else in these docs. Read them twice.

1. **Pushing `main` is shipping.** Both repos deploy to production on Vercel as soon as `main` is pushed. Never push without the owner's yes.
2. **Convex deploys separately.** `npx convex deploy` changes the live backend for every app at once. Treat it like a push to `main`.
3. **Secrets never leave their vault.** API keys and tokens live in Vercel and Convex environment settings and the team password manager. Never in git, these docs, chat, screenshots or the browser console.
4. **Nothing goes out without a human yes.** Social posts, emails and broadcasts are approved one by one by an admin. That includes anything an AI assistant drafts.
5. **No data-changing jobs on production without asking.** Backfills, migrations and one-off `npx convex run` calls against prod need the owner's okay first.
6. **Earn links, never buy them.** No paid links, link networks or link swaps. See [SEO](/seo).

<Callout kind="warning">If you are unsure whether something reaches production or real people, assume it does and ask first.</Callout>

## How to ask good questions

- Say what you expected, what happened, and where (which app, which page, which environment).
- Link the docs page or file you were reading.
- For anything touching production, write down the exact command or click you plan to make before you make it.
